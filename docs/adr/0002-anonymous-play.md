# Anonymous play and a 13+ listing

Orangeutan ships with no name and no avatar. The gear on Home leads to the Privacy policy and to Support. Play time stays on the device. The Score stays the only shared number. Sound, motion, and type size follow the system. There is no preferences menu.

The listing is a logic puzzle. The privacy policy says the game is not directed to children under 13. App Store Connect overrides the calculated 4+ to 13+, and the questionnaire lists only content the game has. Google Play's target audience is 13 and older. The app stays out of the Kids Category and Designed for Families, and it never asks the player's age. The orangutan stays. Until ranking, nothing leaves the device except the system share sheet, and the privacy label is Data Not Collected.

We rejected a name in this version, a preferences menu, a 4+ listing, and a birthday question. A birthday would be data about children. A 4+ listing would leave parental controls open to younger accounts. Inventing mature-content answers to force 13+ would make the listing false.

A later ranking, milestone 12, starts only when the owner asks. It uses one generated Nickname the player can change, on iOS and Android. A random player id stays on the device. Two phones are two rows. Wiping the app starts a new Nickname. Each Level has a Rank ordered by that Level's Play time. The Celebration shows those Nicknames when the board has loaded, and still offers Next when it has not. The gear then also offers rename and delete. That milestone filters generated and renamed Nicknames, and includes report, block, and a published contact. That is when data leaves the device and the privacy label changes. We rejected Game Center because it cannot name a player on Android. The Nickname word list, where the server runs, and who answers reports wait for that milestone. The gear's layout and the Rank on the Celebration need a preview before any visual change.
