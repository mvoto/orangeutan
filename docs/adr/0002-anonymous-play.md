# Anonymous play and a 13+ listing

Orangeutan ships with a generated Nickname stored in the on-device save, and no avatar. The word list is in the app. The player can change that Nickname. A name the filter rejects does not replace it. Home greets the player with that Nickname. The gear on Home leads to Settings. Settings leads to the Nickname and to the Privacy policy and to Support. Play time stays on the device. The Score stays the only shared number. No Rank is published yet. Sound, motion, and type size follow the system. There is no preferences menu.

The listing is a logic puzzle. The privacy policy says the game is not directed to children under 13. App Store Connect overrides the calculated 4+ to 13+, and the questionnaire lists only content the game has. Google Play's target audience is 13 and older. The app stays out of the Kids Category and Designed for Families, and it never asks the player's age. The orangutan stays. Until ranking, nothing leaves the device except the system share sheet, and the privacy label is Data Not Collected.

We rejected a preferences menu, a 4+ listing, and a birthday question. A birthday would be data about children. A 4+ listing would leave parental controls open to younger accounts. Inventing mature-content answers to force 13+ would make the listing false.

A later ranking, milestone 12, starts only when the owner asks. The Nickname word list is already in the app, and the Nickname is already stored in the on-device save. It uses that generated Nickname the player can change, on iOS and Android. A random player id stays on the device. Two phones are two rows. Wiping the app starts a new Nickname. Each Level has a Rank ordered by that Level's Play time. The Celebration shows those Nicknames when the board has loaded, and still offers Next when it has not. The gear then also offers delete. That milestone filters generated and renamed Nicknames, and includes report, block, and a published contact. That is when data leaves the device and the privacy label changes. We rejected Game Center because it cannot name a player on Android. Where the server runs, and who answers reports, still wait for that milestone. The gear's layout and the Rank on the Celebration need a preview before any visual change.
