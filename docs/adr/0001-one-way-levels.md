# One-way Levels

The player moves through a long sequence of Puzzles, from a 4×4 with no Edge clues to larger boards, and never goes back. A solved Level is retired. PLAY resumes the current unsolved Level. Between Levels, a Celebration offers Next, or Home after the last Level. The Score and Share appear only then. The pixel-art picture stays a later reward. It is not the Celebration.

We rejected a Level list and replay. They would keep hundreds of finished Puzzles on screen, and the owner wants only the current one. We rejected undo and reset for the same reason: the board remembers, and a solved Level is already gone. Hint can be added later. We rejected shipping a short curated pack. The sequence has at least 200 Levels, each with one logical solution, and it keeps growing past that while that bar holds.
